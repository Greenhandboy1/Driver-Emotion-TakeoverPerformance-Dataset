# Driver-Emotion-TakeoverPerformance-Dataset
Dataset description for the study “Predicting Driver Takeover Performance under the Influence of Emotion Using Millimeter-Wave Radar”

# Data Availability Update

We regret to inform the research community that the dataset previously made publicly available when our article was made available online is no longer publicly accessible.

Although the participants whose data were shared initially provided informed consent for public data sharing, following the online publication of the article, some participants expressed concerns regarding the continued public sharing of their physiological data, particularly heart rate data. These concerns primarily stemmed from the potentially sensitive nature of individual-level physiological data. For example, such data may provide insights into individuals' health status, raising concerns about the unintended disclosure of personal health information.

To respect participants' concerns, protect their privacy, and prevent similar concerns or potential disputes arising from the continued public sharing of other participants' data, we have adopted a precautionary approach and decided to temporarily suspend public access to the entire dataset.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this decision may cause to researchers and other interested parties. We appreciate your understanding and remain committed to protecting participants' privacy and upholding ethical standards in scientific research.
